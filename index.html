Meet your orthodontist

Dr. Dante Gonzales, DMD, MSD

“When I talk about braces, I don't just speak from years of training — I wore them myself. I know how it feels, and seeing the confidence in someone's new smile reminds me of how I felt when mine came off.”

UCLA. Tufts School of Dental Medicine, Summa Cum Laude. Orthodontic residency and a master's at University of the Pacific, where he later returned as an associate clinical professor. A Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics, and the sole orthodontist on the editorial advisory board of Sharecare — the health company founded by Dr. Oz and WebMD's Jeff Arnold.
