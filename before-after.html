Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about smile transformations, Invisalign®, braces, and treatment timelines.

How long does the average smile transformation take?

Treatment duration varies based on the complexity of your case, but most transformations take between 9 to 18 months. Minor cosmetic alignments can take as little as 6 months, while complex bite corrections may take up to 24 months. With our accelerated orthodontic technology and precision 3D digital planning, we consistently deliver results faster and with fewer appointments.

Can Invisalign® fix severe crowding, overbites, and crossbites as well as braces?

Yes! As a Top 1% Diamond Plus Invisalign Provider, Dr. Dante Gonzales specializes in treating complex orthodontic conditions using clear aligners — including severe crowding, deep overbites, crossbites, and open bites — often without needing teeth extractions or jaw surgery.

What happens at my complimentary initial smile consultation?

Your free Gonzales Smile Assessment includes a comprehensive exam by Dr. Gonzales, digital photographs, an iTero® 3D digital scan of your teeth, a simulated preview of your final smile, and a discussion of all treatment options, timelines, insurance benefits, and flexible payment plans.

What is the Gonzales Lifetime Smile Guarantee?

We stand behind our clinical results for life. If your teeth ever shift at any point in the future due to lost retainers or natural changes, Dr. Gonzales will align your teeth back at a minimal fee. Your confident smile is guaranteed for a lifetime.

What payment plans and insurance options are available?

We believe financial considerations should never prevent you or your child from having a healthy, beautiful smile. We offer 0% interest financing, low monthly payment options, down payment flexibility, family discounts, and we accept and maximize all major orthodontic insurance plans.

Are same-day appointments and same-day treatment starts available?

Yes! We value your busy schedule. Both our Dublin and Tracy orthodontic offices offer same-day consultations and same-day treatment starts so you can begin your journey to a confident smile without waiting.
