What is Obstructive Sleep Apnea & Why It Matters

When you lie down, your throat muscles and soft tissue may relax too far back, the airway narrows and you stop breathing for a few seconds.

That is typical of obstructive sleep apnea. There is also central sleep apnea where the brain doesn’t send signals to breathe, but our clinic more often treats the obstructive type.

These sleep-related issues can cause loud snoring, morning headaches, or waking with a choking feeling. Over weeks and months the low oxygen levels strain your heart and lungs. Without accurate diagnosis and care, the risk of heart attack, stroke or worse rises.

Our Treatment Approach in Dublin CA

We start off by reviewing the results of your sleep study from your medical doctor. Then we customise treatment options with the goal of restorative sleep and better quality of life. Our dental team works with your sleep physician or respiratory medicine specialist if needed.

How We Design the Right Sleep Apnea Treatment

Our team begins by reviewing your sleep study results or arranging one if you haven’t had it done. Once diagnosed, we suggest treatment approaches based on your pattern — whether it’s obstructive sleep apnea, central sleep apnea, or mixed types. A small change in jaw alignment can often make a big difference.

Most treating sleep apnea cases here rely on oral appliance therapy. It’s a custom device you wear while sleeping, a simple oral appliance that holds your jaw forward, keeping your airway open throughout the night.

The idea is to prevent your tongue and soft tissues from collapsing backward. Feels strange at first, but within a few nights, patients often say they slept peacefully for the first time in years. That’s how it should be.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What causes sleep apnea?

It happens when throat muscles and soft tissue block the airway for a few seconds during sleep. This interrupts breathing and lowers oxygen levels.

2. How does Dr Dante Gonzales treat sleep apnea?

We use oral appliances that keep the jaw forward to open the airway and improve nighttime breathing. Other treatment options are reviewed if needed.

3. Can untreated sleep apnea cause other health issues?

Yes. It may lead to high blood pressure, heart attack, stroke, and daytime irritability. Proper treatment can restore healthy sleep and energy.

4. Is sleep apnea treatment painful or uncomfortable?

Not at all. Most patients find our treatments gentle and easy to adapt to. The oral appliance fits comfortably and doesn’t interfere with regular sleep. Within a few nights, it often feels completely natural.

5. How soon can I expect results from treatment?

Many patients notice better sleep and more energy within the first few weeks. Consistent use of the appliance or other recommended therapies leads to steady, lasting improvement.
