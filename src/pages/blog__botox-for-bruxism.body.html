Teeth grinding and jaw clenching are among the most damaging and difficult habits to stop because they happen without conscious thought. Bruxism the involuntary clenching, grinding, or gnashing of teeth that affects millions of patients wears down tooth enamel.

The Causes and Consequences of Bruxism

Stress, sleep apnea, and bite misalignments often trigger nocturnal grinding, leading to fractured teeth, damaged restorations, and painful facial muscles.

How Masseter Injections Break the Cycle

Botox relaxes the masseter muscles, preventing excessive clenching forces while maintaining full natural jaw mobility and facial expression.

Combining Botox with Orthodontic Alignment

Addressing both muscular tension with Botox and dental alignment with orthodontic care provides the most durable long-term solution against tooth wear.
