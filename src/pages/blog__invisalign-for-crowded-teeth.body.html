Crowded teeth are one of the most common dental issues orthodontists treat and one of the most common reasons patients begin their Invisalign journey at Dante Gonzales Orthodontics.

Why Crowding Happens

A mismatch between jaw size and tooth dimensions causes teeth to overlap, twist, and shift out of the dental arch.

Clear Aligners vs. Braces for Crowding

Invisalign uses calibrated aligner trays to guide each tooth into position while making brushing and flossing significantly easier than traditional brackets.

Long-Term Health Benefits of Alignment

Aligning crowded teeth eliminates hard-to-clean food traps, drastically reducing your risk of gum inflammation, cavities, and uneven enamel wear.
