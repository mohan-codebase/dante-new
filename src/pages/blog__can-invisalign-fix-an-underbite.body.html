An underbite is more than a cosmetic concern. When the lower teeth protrude beyond the upper front teeth, it creates a misaligned bite that places uneven stress on the jaw joints.

Types of Underbites (Dental vs. Skeletal)

Dental underbites arise from misdirected teeth, whereas skeletal underbites involve differences in upper and lower jaw growth.

How Invisalign Corrects Dental Underbites

By combining aligner stages with Class III intermaxillary elastics, Dr. Gonzales gently retracts lower teeth and expands the upper arch.

Restoring Function and Facial Balance

Correcting an underbite improves chewing efficiency, eliminates speech impediments, and protects front teeth from chipping.
