If you regularly experience headaches that no amount of pain medication fully resolves, your jaw may be the root cause. TMJ headaches, headaches caused or worsened by temporomandibular joint TMJ dysfunction, are among the most frequently misdiagnosed causes of chronic head pain.

The Neural and Muscular Connection

The trigeminal nerve innervates both the jaw joints and the cranial musculature. Hyperactive jaw clenching radiates pain upward into the temples, forehead, and neck.

Identifying TMJ Headaches vs. Traditional Migraines

TMJ headaches typically peak in the morning upon waking and coincide with jaw tightness, clicking joints, or limited mouth opening.
