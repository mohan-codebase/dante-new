Botox for TMJ is one of the most effective and increasingly popular treatment options for patients suffering from TMJ pain, teeth grinding, jaw tension, and the chronic headaches that temporomandibular joint disorder causes in daily life.

How Botox Alleviates Jaw Tension

By temporarily decreasing contractions in the large masseter and temporalis muscles, Botox relieves chronic clenching without affecting your ability to chew or speak normally.

Unit Requirements & Typical Pricing

Patients generally require 20 to 50 units per side depending on muscle bulk. Sessions typically range from $500 to $1,200 and provide relief for 3 to 6 months.
