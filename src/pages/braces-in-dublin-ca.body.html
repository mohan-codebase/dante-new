What are Traditional Braces and How They Work?

Traditional braces use small brackets bonded to your teeth with a special adhesive. Wires and elastic bands apply gentle pressure to move each tooth into its desired position. Over time, your bite improves, and your smile begins to line up perfectly. Feels slow at first, but it’s steady progress.

We use both traditional metal braces and more discreet options like clear ceramic braces. Metal braces remain the strongest and most reliable choice, especially for teens and adults with more complex orthodontic needs. The strength of metal allows precise tooth movement even in deep or challenging cases.

It’s simple really, braces fix what’s not aligned. But how we get there depends on your teeth, your mouth, and your smile goals.

The Importance of Good Oral Hygiene

Wearing braces means you must maintain good oral hygiene daily. Food particles often get stuck near brackets, wires, and elastic bands. If not cleaned, they cause plaque and affect oral health.

We teach patients to brush 3–4 times per day, use floss threaders to floss once daily, and rinse regularly. For adults, especially those using metal braces, keeping the mouth clean prevents gum swelling. And for teens, it’s a chance to build habits early. Maintain good oral hygiene, and the final smile looks even brighter.

How Braces Fix Misaligned Teeth

Braces fix misaligned teeth by applying gentle pressure over time. This moves your teeth into the desired position, allowing your bite and jaw to align properly. It’s not just cosmetic. Correcting your bite improves chewing, speech, and overall oral health.

We sometimes see front teeth pushed forward or lower teeth crowding. Small metal adjustments with elastic bands can solve that easily. For adults, fixed braces work especially well since they need steady, consistent movement. Every adjustment brings teeth closer to their perfect alignment. The patience pays off.

Keeping Gums and Teeth Clean During Braces

Braces collect food particles easily. That’s why keeping teeth clean is part of the daily routine. Brushing twice, using floss threaders, and avoiding sticky food keeps your gums healthy.

Our orthodontist often reminds patients that clean teeth move faster. When oral hygiene stays consistent, the treatment time shortens. Neglecting it can cause white marks or even tooth loss later. So we always say, take those five minutes every night. It really matters.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How long does braces treatment usually take in Dublin CA?

It depends on your case, but most patients complete braces in 12 to 24 months. Regular check-ups help keep treatment on schedule.

2. Are ceramic braces as effective as metal braces?

Yes, they work just like metal braces but look more natural. Many adults choose ceramic braces for a subtle appearance.

3. Can I get a free consultation before starting braces?

Yes. Dr Dante Gonzales Orthodontics offers a free consultation in Dublin CA to review your teeth and suggest the best treatment plan for you

4. Do braces hurt after getting them?

You might feel mild pressure or soreness for the first few days as your teeth begin to move. It’s a normal part of the process and usually fades quickly. Most patients say it feels more like tightness than pain, and over-the-counter relief works just fine.

5. Can adults get braces even if they’ve had dental work before?

Absolutely. Many of our adult patients have fillings, crowns, or even implants. We design your orthodontic plan around your existing dental work to ensure everything aligns safely and comfortably. It’s never too late to improve your smile.
