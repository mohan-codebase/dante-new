Temporomandibular joint TMJ disorders affect tens of millions of people and produce some of the most disruptive and difficult-to-diagnose pain conditions in dental medicine. The jaw joint and surrounding jaw muscles are involved in virtually every movement of daily life, from eating and talking to yawning and swallowing, which means that TMJ pain affects function in ways most other joint disorders simply do not.

Understanding TMJ Dysfunction

TMJ disorders arise from jaw misalignment, muscle hyperactivity, bruxism (clenching/grinding), or structural damage within the joint disc.

Non-Invasive Therapies & Custom Splints

Custom occlusal splints and night guards decompress the joint and shield teeth from nocturnal grinding forces, offering immediate relief from acute strain.

Therapeutic Botox & Orthodontic Realignment

Targeted Botox injections relax hyperactive masseter muscles, while orthodontic treatment addresses underlying bite discrepancies to deliver lasting relief.
