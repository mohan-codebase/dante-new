If you've spent years thinking about straightening your teeth but couldn't picture yourself wearing traditional metal braces to a client meeting or a first date, you are not alone. Invisalign treatment for adults has become one of the most requested orthodontic treatments in the country, and for good reason.

Why Adults Choose Clear Aligners

Invisalign aligners are virtually invisible, removable for eating and brushing, and engineered with SmartTrack material for predictable, comfortable tooth movement without brackets or wires.

Typical Adult Treatment Timeline

Most adult cases take between 6 and 18 months depending on the extent of crowding, spacing, or bite correction required. Digital 3D scans allow you to preview your projected outcome before you even begin.

Investment & Financing

Adult Invisalign costs are comparable to traditional braces and can often be paid using FSA/HSA accounts, dental insurance benefits, and custom zero-interest monthly payment plans.
