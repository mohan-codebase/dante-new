If your child has a gap between their front teeth, a bite that doesn't quite line up, or you've simply noticed crowded teeth coming in, you have probably asked yourself the same question every parent eventually asks: what is the best age for braces?

The Age 7 Checkup Recommendation

The American Association of Orthodontists (AAO) recommends that children have their first orthodontic evaluation around age 7. By this age, the first adult molars and incisors have erupted, allowing Dr. Dante Gonzales to detect subtle developmental issues early.

Phase 1 vs. Comprehensive Phase 2 Treatment

Early interceptive treatment (Phase 1) guides jaw growth, creates room for crowded erupting teeth, and prevents more invasive corrections later. Comprehensive treatment (Phase 2) is usually completed during the pre-teen and teen years once all permanent teeth have arrived.

It's Never Too Late: Adult Orthodontics

While childhood and adolescence offer natural growth advantages, healthy teeth can be moved at any age. Over 25% of our orthodontic patients in Dublin and Tracy are adults achieving the smile they always wanted.
