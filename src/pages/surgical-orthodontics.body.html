Surgical Orthodontics

Surgical Orthodontics in Dublin and Tracy, CA, aka orthognathic surgery, simply means “straight jaws.” All kinds of symptoms can result when jaws don’t align. Do you suffer from any of these…?

Surgical Orthodontics in Dublin and Tracy, CA, For Proper Jaw Alignment

  • Trouble chewing or biting food
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Chronic jaw or jaw joint (TMJ/TMD) pain and headaches
  • Excessive wearing of the teeth
  • Open bite (space between the upper and lower teeth when the mouth is closed)
  • “Lopsided” facial appearance from the front or side
  • Facial injury or birth defect
  • Receding chin
  • Protruding jaw
  • Inability to make the lips meet without straining
  • Chronic mouth breathing and dry mouth
  • Breathing problems while sleeping, including snoring (sleep apnea)

Many people don’t realize that we can correct their problems with surgical orthodontics in Dublin and Tracy, CA, coupled with orthodontics afterwards to realign the teeth. Dr. Gonzales will work very closely with you to set your teeth up properly for the surgery. The surgery can help you to chew, speak, and breathe better. Frequently, they can also help to enhance your appearance.
