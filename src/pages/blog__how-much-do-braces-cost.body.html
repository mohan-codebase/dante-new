Whether you're researching orthodontic treatment for a child, considering adult treatment for yourself, or simply trying to plan your family's budget for the year ahead, one question comes up first: how much do braces cost?

Average Cost of Braces in California

The cost of orthodontic treatment varies depending on complexity, treatment duration, and the type of braces chosen (metal, clear ceramic, or lingual). On average in Northern California, comprehensive braces treatment typically ranges from $3,500 to $7,500 before insurance or flexible financing.

What Factors Influence the Total Price?

Several clinical factors determine your investment: severity of crowding or bite misalignment, estimated time required to complete treatment, and whether Phase 1 early interceptive treatment is needed for young children.
